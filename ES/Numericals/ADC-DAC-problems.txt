1. An ADC has a full-scale ±2.5 V input range (bipolar), 12-bit resolution, and uses straight binary coding for positive voltages (two’s-complement is not used).
a) What is the LSB (quantization step) ΔV?
b) What analog voltage corresponds to the digital code 0x800 (hex)? Show your working.

2. A 14-bit ADC is used to sample a full-scale sinewave.
a) Compute the quantization SNR in dB (assume an ideal ADC and a full-scale sine).
b) If the measured SNR of the system is 77.0 dB, compute the Effective Number Of Bits (ENOB).

3. Design requirement: digitize an audio signal (20 Hz–20 kHz) with dynamic range of 100 dB and where anti-aliasing must be minimal in the passband. Which ADC architecture is most appropriate: SAR, delta-sigma (Σ–Δ), pipeline, or flash? Explain with reasons and pick a target resolution (bits) and sample rate.

2. DAC Output Equation

A 12-bit unipolar DAC uses a 2.5 V reference and outputs voltage according to

Vout = (D / (2^12 - 1)) * Vref

a) Compute VOUT for digital input  D=2048
b) If the DAC were bipolar (±2.5 V range), what would  VOUT be for the same code?

3. Linearity and Accuracy

b) Calculate the maximum absolute output error (in volts) for a 0–10 V range.
